Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Plantify, we collect the room images you upload to perform computer vision analysis, detect lighting parameters, and generate plant placement models. If you create an account, we collect your name, email address, username, and interior plant preferences.

2. How We Use Image Data

Your uploaded photos are processed exclusively to measure natural illumination, identify surface bounding boxes, and synthesize photorealistic preview transformations using AI. We never sell your photos or private room images to third-party data brokers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete any saved room scan or permanently delete your entire Plantify account at any time from your Account Settings page.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@plantify.ai.
