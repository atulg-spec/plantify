5. Actionable Plant Care Guide

Straightforward, practical maintenance guidelines designed to prevent overwatering and promote steady growth.

{{ analysis.care_routine_summary|default:"Weekly Inspection Rhythm" }}

Watering Discipline

Insert your index finger 2 inches into the soil. Water only if soil feels completely dry at that depth. Never water on a blind calendar schedule.

Sunlight & Windows

Maintain plants in bright to medium indirect light. Shield tender tropical foliage from harsh direct afternoon sunbeams through sheer curtains.

Drainage & Soil

Ensure all containers have bottom drainage holes. Empty saucers 15 minutes after watering so root systems never stay submerged in standing water.

Seasonal Adjustments

In spring & summer, plants drink faster during active growth. In winter dormancy, cut watering frequency in half and halt fertilizing.
